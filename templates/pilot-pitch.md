# Pilot pitch

For a 30-day AI pilot at your current job. One page; fifteen minutes to present.

## The problem, in hours

Who does what by hand, how often, and what it costs per week in hours and money.

## The pilot

One tool, one workflow, 30 days, budget under $<amount>. What it will and will not do.

## Data and risk

Where the data goes, what never leaves, which provider terms apply, what a bad output costs and how it is caught.

## How we will know

Three numbers at day 30: task success on a golden set, cost per task, hours saved per week. The baseline for each today.

## What I need

Access, a reviewer, and the title conversation if it works.
