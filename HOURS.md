# Hours

The course is sized at **200 guided hours** (COURSE-SPEC.md). The table allocates that budget per module and sums exactly. At about 10 hours a week that is 20 weeks; full-time learners can halve the calendar, not the hours. The guided hours are the instruction, labs, targets and the first build of each project. The real-user periods, the job search and the track depth after the core are scoped in the modules but not counted, because the evidence says the whole transition takes 18 months to 3 years and no honest budget compresses that.

## How the numbers were made

The 500-hour first allocation took the midpoints of the practitioner ranges in the 2026 roadmap research. The 200-hour budget scales each module by the same factor and rounds, keeping the proportions the evidence supported (the model from the inside and the measurement stage carry more than half of the hours).
